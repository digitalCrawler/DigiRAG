# Acme Corporation - Global Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Welcome to Acme Corporation
Welcome to our company! We are committed to fostering an innovative, inclusive, and high-performance culture. This document outlines company-wide policies, guidelines, and benefits.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policies

### 2.1 Annual Vacation
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual vacation per calendar year. Vacation accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service. A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the following calendar year, expiring on March 31st.

### 2.2 Parental Leave
Acme provides up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all eligible parents following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months following the qualifying event.

### 2.3 Sabbatical Program
Employees who complete five (5) consecutive years of full-time service with Acme are eligible for a 4-week fully paid sabbatical. Sabbaticals require department head approval at least three months in advance to ensure coverage.

## 3. Remote Work & Home Office Equipment
Acme embraces a flexible remote-first working model. Every full-time remote employee receives:
- A one-time home office setup reimbursement of up to $1,500.
- A monthly internet and utilities stipend of $80.
- Company-issued Apple MacBook Pro or Dell XPS workstation refreshed every 3 years.

## 4. Information Security & Data Classification
All data at Acme is classified into one of three tiers:
1. **Public:** Information approved for public release.
2. **Internal:** Business information intended solely for internal employees.
3. **Confidential & Restricted:** Customer personal data, source code, financial records, and cryptographic secrets. 

Restricted data must never be transmitted over unencrypted communication channels or pasted into unauthorized public LLMs.

## 5. Travel & Expense Reimbursement
Travel for company business must be booked through the Acme corporate travel portal. Daily meal allowances are capped at $90 per day ($20 breakfast, $30 lunch, $40 dinner). Receipts are mandatory for any individual expense exceeding $25.
